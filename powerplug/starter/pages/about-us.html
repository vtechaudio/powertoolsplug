Our story

Power Tools Plug Kenya was founded with a simple objective: to make quality tools and industrial equipment accessible to professionals and everyday users across Kenya.

We saw that many customers struggle to find reliable equipment and clear, honest product information. Our business was built to be a supplier customers can buy from with confidence — a real business with a physical location, knowledgeable staff and clear customer policies.

Today we supply equipment for construction, manufacturing, agriculture, engineering, automotive workshops, plumbing, electrical installations, fabrication, renewable-energy projects and home improvement. We select our stock to help customers work more efficiently, safely and productively.

We deliver across Kenya using trusted courier partners and rider services.
