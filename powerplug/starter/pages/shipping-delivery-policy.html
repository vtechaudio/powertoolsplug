At Power Tools Plug Kenya we deliver power tools, solar equipment, generators and accessories to customers across Kenya. This policy explains where we deliver, how long it takes, what it costs and what to do if anything goes wrong. Last updated: July 2026.

Where we deliver

We deliver countrywide within Kenya:

  • Nairobi & metropolitan area (CBD, Westlands, Industrial Area, Thika Road, Rongai, Ruaka and surrounding estates) – doorstep and rider delivery.
  • Major towns (Mombasa, Kisumu, Nakuru, Eldoret, Nyeri, Meru, Machakos, Kisii, Kericho and others) – courier delivery.
  • All other regions – via trusted couriers to the nearest agent, town or pickup point.
  • In-store pickup at our shop: Magomano House, 1st Floor, Room 10D, Tom Mboya Street, Nairobi.

Delivery options & timelines

Orders are processed during business hours, Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Orders placed on Sundays or public holidays are processed the next business day. Timelines below begin once your order and delivery details are confirmed.

  • Nairobi doorstep / rider: same-day delivery for orders confirmed before 5:00 PM, otherwise the next business day.
  • Pickup point / agent collection (e.g. Pickup Mtaani): 1–2 business days within Nairobi, 2–3 business days upcountry.
  • Courier to major towns (G4S, Wells Fargo, Fargo Courier or reputable coach parcel services): 1–3 business days after dispatch.
  • Remote and other regions: 2–5 business days after dispatch.
  • In-store pickup: ready the same day once we confirm your order and stock.

Delivery charges

  • Within Nairobi: a flat delivery fee of KES 300.
  • Outside Nairobi (upcountry / courier): a flat delivery fee of KES 500. For heavy or oversized items such as generators, compressors and large solar panels, delivery is quoted by the courier based on weight and size and confirmed before dispatch.
  • In-store pickup: free at our shop on Tom Mboya Street, Nairobi.

Your exact delivery charge is shown or confirmed before dispatch, so there are no surprises on arrival.

Order confirmation & tracking

After you place an order, we confirm availability and delivery details by phone, WhatsApp (+254 708 777192) or email. For courier shipments we share the courier name and tracking or waybill number so you can follow your parcel to your town or pickup point.

Receiving your order

Please inspect your item on delivery. For fragile or high-value equipment, confirm the package is sealed and undamaged before you accept it. If anything is wrong, report it immediately so we can put it right (see our Return & Refund Policy and Warranty Policy).

Missed or failed deliveries

If our rider or courier cannot reach you because of an incorrect address, an unavailable recipient or an unreachable phone, we will attempt to reschedule. Repeated failed deliveries or refused parcels may attract a re-delivery fee. Parcels held at a courier agent should be collected within that agent's holding period.

Delays beyond our control

Delivery times are good-faith estimates. We are not liable for delays caused by circumstances outside our control, including weather, traffic, courier backlogs, incorrect delivery details or public holidays. We will always keep you updated.

Damaged, defective or incorrect items

If your item arrives damaged, defective or not as ordered, contact us within 24–48 hours of delivery with photos. We will arrange a replacement, repair or resolution in line with our Return & Refund Policy and Warranty Policy.
